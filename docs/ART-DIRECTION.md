# Cinematic concept artwork

Three generated bitmap assets support the film. Their visual descriptions are reconstruction specifications for future iterations, not a verbatim generation transcript. The source files and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [media provenance](../data/media-provenance.json).

All three use a 16:9 cinematic composition, physically based material treatment, realistic titanium and smoked glass, and the original Cognitive Overdrive palette: near-black `#070909`, cream `#eee9dd`, lime `#d7f44c`, pink `#ff349d`, and cyan `#58c9ed`. Keep the principal structure on the right, with a darker left field for the short narrative headline. Avoid text, invented logos, watermarks, human subjects, and fabricated performance metrics.

## Edge

Create a realistic rugged titanium edge computer and inspection camera mounted on a plinth at a blue-hour industrial site. Show a short local cyan/lime data path and a much longer distant pink path toward a datacenter. Use detailed connectors, vents, realistic surface reflections, atmospheric depth and restrained volumetric light. The idea is inference close to the point of need. Asset: `public/renders/edge.png`.

## Compute

Create an exploded shared platform: one broad smoked-glass application plane floating over four distinct interchangeable CPU/accelerator cartridges. Use brushed black titanium, realistic heatsinks, circuit traces, glass reflections and lime/cyan/pink signal paths rising into the common plane. Emphasize stable application contracts and qualified target choice without using any vendor logo or performance ranking. Asset: `public/renders/compute.png`.

## Launchpad

Create a modular delivery system of glass and titanium capsules assembled on a gantry and rail into repeatable workstations. Use physically based lighting and materials, fine mechanical detail, and the same restrained brand accents. The scene should communicate repeatable provisioning and handoff. Asset: `public/renders/launchpad.png`.

These are illustrative concept renders and are labeled as such on screen. They do not establish deployment, measured results, or product appearance. The separate `SignalSculpture` component uses projected 3D geometry, depth sorting and time-driven particles; architecture flows and proof gates are code-driven animation. Original project clips retain their own evidence labels.
