# Evidence behind the portfolio

The portfolio demonstrates the engineering and communication of technical experiences. It distinguishes the implementation described by a source repository, recorded evidence from that project's own validation, and what the current portfolio session actually executes.

## The versioned inventory

[data/portfolio-evidence.json](../data/portfolio-evidence.json) records 27 cases, their audiences, problem, workflow, reuse, maturity and claim boundaries. Its review date is **26 October 2026**.

The inventory was assembled through read-only documentation and source-layout inspection. Public repository visibility was checked separately through GitHub metadata. It includes 24 confirmed public repository links. AERIS and the two sales-entry experiences have no public source link because public access was not confirmed.

No underlying service or test suite was run as part of that inventory. Repository links are navigation references, not immutable runtime qualification. A link being public does not establish that every locally inspected change has been published there.

The full working research contained private/local provenance. The version committed here excludes absolute workstation paths, private endpoints, credentials, raw operational evidence and the restricted Cascade Production Proof case. The public Cascade Compression case remains.

Jonathan Kershaw's displayed name is grounded in explicit Triforce project authorship documentation. Upstream projects and third-party components retain their own authorship; for example, the Praxis case describes the lab and integration experience, not authorship of the upstream gateway.

## What the labels mean

- **Implemented demo / platform / service:** application or service code and the described delivery assets exist in the inspected source. This label is not a production certification.
- **Reference implementation:** an executable integration pattern or learning baseline, with additional operating requirements remaining.
- **Documented pilot:** the project documentation describes a deployed pilot. The portfolio does not independently monitor its current health or outcomes.
- **Validated local reference:** recorded checks cover explicitly bounded local, connected-service or same-host installation behavior.
- **Rehearsal / factory candidate:** synthetic or deterministic evidence demonstrates a contract and experience. Destination-specific live execution, certification and promotion remain separate.
- **Alpha / incubating OSS / research candidate:** implemented mechanics or research contracts exist, while broader release, effectiveness or operating qualification remains open.
- **Runnable concept:** an interactive conceptual experience exists; real-world integration and efficacy are not implied.

Keep the concise badge readable. Put the meaningful limitations in “What I built,” “How it works” or “How it is consumed” so the viewer can assess the case.

## Current portfolio execution

The architecture chapter explains source-documented Triforce responsibilities. It performs no agent discovery, model inference, MCP invocation or cluster query.

The proof chapter runs a deterministic browser policy with three conditions: complete evidence, missing evidence and denied review authority. A review handoff requires matching records and an explicit authority grant. The displayed result does not approve a transaction or execute an action.

Every exported proof record carries `source: "SIMULATION"`. The export describes only this session's browser-generated observations. Its timestamps are record creation times, not latency measurements. The return chapter reports cases inspected and simulations performed during the current in-memory session.

## Boundaries worth preserving

**Cost-Aware Inference:** the cost measure is an execution proxy with visible assumptions. It excludes full infrastructure and operating costs. The score belongs to a named case; a placement comparison changes policy plus selected model, not hardware alone.

**The Great CPU Bake Off:** a separate creative frontend and film treatment reuses the Cost-Aware proof API. Its culinary analogy changes communication, not evidence: actual responses, model identities, source labels and named-case checks remain inspectable. Rehearsal values cannot receive live-comparison awards. The source retains campaign-title/typeface review before external launch.

**Edge:** the development transport is simulated SMS and the included local model is a baseline. Conference-assistant behavior is not evidence of medical, humanitarian or field efficacy.

**Triforce:** historical benchmark numbers belong to their documented environments and conditions. The portfolio does not replay or remeasure them. Roadmap modules remain roadmap.

**Launchpad and StarGate:** implementation, deployment packaging and pilot documentation do not establish current fleet health, attendance, customer adoption or quantified operating savings.

**Cascade:** the public OSS artifact and restricted local production-proof implementation differ. Historical private percentages cannot qualify the public candidate. The portfolio claims no zero-miss behavior or production effectiveness.

**GCL, Passport, Ledger and Earned Authority:** evidence, proposal, permission, execution and independent outcome remain separate responsibilities. A ledger receipt proves an assertion was submitted and retained under its verification rules; it does not make the assertion true. Local milestone counts do not establish business value or general production readiness.

**Advanced learning paths:** Sovereign AI, Virtualization + AI and Agentic AI candidates retain their rehearsal, certification and authority limits. Signed packaging does not establish live confidential inference, VM placement, fleet behavior or authorization to operate.

**AERIS:** the interactive experience is a rehearsal concept with 3D visualization and a persistent decision record. It connects no physical robot, camera, JEPA model or accelerator. Separate recorded experiments do not establish learned-planning advantage or physical inspection qualification.

## Hardware breadth and delivery reuse

[data/hardware-evidence.json](../data/hardware-evidence.json) preserves the distinct evidence behind the Intel, AMD, Arm and NVIDIA story. Its public-safe copy is served at `/evidence/hardware-evidence.json`, including original capture timestamps, hardware identity declarations, source states and SHA-256 hashes for the retained AMD artifacts. No hardware is connected to the portfolio.

- **Intel:** Triforce and Cost-Aware document Xeon CPU and Gaudi inference paths. Their recorded measurements keep their original environment and conditions.
- **AMD:** retained Cost-Aware records from 8 October identify observed EPYC 9965 and Instinct MI350P hardware. Later records pass their named-case checks; earlier truncation failures are retained. Hardware support status is explicitly unknown. The public repository exists, but these newer artifact paths were unavailable through its public contents endpoint during review. Only sanitized metadata and hashes are copied here.
- **NVIDIA:** the public [fleet whitepaper](https://github.com/jkershawrh/llm-d-fleet/blob/main/docs/whitepaper/fleet-llm-d-whitepaper.md#556-3-cluster-cascade-soak-august-2026) describes a historical three-cluster Intel CPU / H100 NVL run. The old topology and signing contract are explicitly superseded. This is historical execution evidence, not a current cross-vendor comparison.
- **Arm:** the public [release evidence package](https://github.com/jkershawrh/llm-d-fleet/blob/main/docs/upstream/reviewer-evidence-package.md) documents ARM64 binaries and multi-architecture images. The local Cost-Aware adapter preserves Arm64 architecture metadata, but native Arm inference qualification remains outstanding. Packaging and functional tests do not establish Arm inference performance.

CPU architecture, vendor, accelerator type, model and runtime are different dimensions. Separate saved runs do not form one simultaneous live bake-off. This portfolio makes no all-vendor qualification or hardware-superiority claim.

**Launchpad** connects this technical work to a repeatable participant experience: **request → provision → validate → participant handoff → reclaim**. Its documented pilot uses direct OpenShift execution, catalog contracts and target-aware cleanup. Current health, attendance and business outcomes require their own evidence.

## Update a claim

1. Identify the exact source and what it actually establishes.
2. Update its inventory entry and the corresponding project card together.
3. Keep the distinction between historical results and current-session observations.
4. Preserve meaningful failure and unavailable cases.
5. Add a public source link only when access is confirmed.
6. Validate the story and inspect the rendered case before sharing.

No portfolio case asserts independently measured revenue, conversion, adoption, productivity, savings or customer impact. Business value is expressed as the decision the experience helps a viewer examine and the reusable capability it exposes.

Narration generation provenance is separate from project evidence. The narration manifest and narration documentation are the source of truth for the active voice preset; historical research settings are not duplicated here.
